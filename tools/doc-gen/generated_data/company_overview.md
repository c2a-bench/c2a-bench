# Skyway Air — Company Overview

## Company at a Glance

**Skyway Air** is a mid-size U.S. hybrid/value airline flying domestic and short-haul international routes from hubs in Nashville (BNA) and Raleigh-Durham (RDU), known for simple fares and friendly service.

Founded in 2015, Skyway is a profitable, privately held airline with approximately 5,500 employees, an all-Airbus A220-family fleet of about 60 aircraft, roughly 90 destinations, and approximately 9 million passengers annually.

## Mission and Vision

- **Mission:** Make flying simple and fair.
- **Vision:** Be the airline people trust when plans change.

## Company Overview

Skyway operates a domestic U.S. network alongside short-haul international service to Canada, Mexico, and the Caribbean. It uses two hubs—Nashville and Raleigh-Durham—to connect passengers across its route network.

The airline sells tickets through its website, mobile app, call centre, and travel agents. It is a hybrid/value carrier: it aims to offer lower prices and simpler choices than major legacy airlines, while providing clearer policies and fairer service than an ultra-low-cost airline.

## Customers Served

Skyway’s customer mix is approximately:

- **70% leisure travelers and families**
- **30% small-business travelers**

Many customers are first-time or occasional flyers. Skyway therefore treats plain-language explanations of fares, policies, fees, and travel options as an important part of the customer experience.

## Product Surface Area and Customer Experience

### Booking and trip management

Customers can book and manage travel through the Skyway website, mobile app, call centre, and travel agents. Digital services include online check-in, mobile boarding passes, flight alerts, and self-service trip changes where eligible.

### Fare types

Skyway offers three clearly differentiated fare types:

- **Basic:** Lowest price; no changes and no checked bag included.
- **Standard:** Changes permitted with a fee; one checked bag included.
- **Flex:** Free changes, refundable travel, and two checked bags included.

### Add-ons

Available add-ons include:

- Advance seat selection
- Extra checked bags
- Priority boarding
- Pets in cabin
- Travel insurance through a third-party partner

### Airport, onboard, and support experience

Skyway provides airport check-in, bag-drop, boarding, and airport service desks. Its onboard offer includes free inflight messaging, paid Wi-Fi, and buy-on-board food and drinks.

Customer support is available through chat, email, phone, and airport desks. The airline also offers proactive notifications and rebooking support during disruptions.

### Loyalty

**Skyway Miles** is Skyway’s loyalty programme, with **Blue**, **Silver**, and **Gold** tiers. The programme supports points earning and redemption, tier benefits, and loyalty partnerships, including a co-branded credit card.

## How the Business and Technology Work

Skyway’s central booking system manages inventory, fares, reservations, payments, and ancillary purchases across direct and indirect sales channels. Airport departure-control systems support check-in, boarding, baggage processing, and flight departures.

A customer-support platform gives agents access to booking and case information across chat, email, phone, and airport interactions. Agents use written customer policies to handle changes, cancellations, refunds, delays, baggage issues, special assistance, and Skyway Miles cases.

Skyway has detailed customer policies that include specific fees, deadlines, eligibility requirements, exceptions, and approval limits. These policies change during the year. The formal policy source is maintained in **Confluence**, while policy changes, edge cases, and exceptions are often discussed in **Slack**. This makes policy governance, access to current guidance, and consistent application across support and operational teams especially important.

## Differentiation

Skyway differentiates itself through:

- **Clear fares:** Basic, Standard, and Flex are designed to make included benefits and restrictions easy to understand.
- **Plain-language policies:** Important for first-time and occasional flyers.
- **“Plans Change” promise:** Every fare includes free cancellation within 24 hours of booking.
- **Fast refunds:** Streamlined refund handling with clear customer status communication.
- **Accessible support:** Digital and human assistance across chat, email, phone, and airport desks.
- **Fair-value positioning:** Lower-cost than legacy carriers while offering clearer policies and more customer-friendly treatment than ultra-low-cost competitors.

## Business Model and Revenue Streams

Skyway’s principal revenue streams are:

- Ticket revenue from Basic, Standard, and Flex fares
- Ancillary revenue from bags, seat selection, priority boarding, pets in cabin, and partner-provided travel insurance
- Loyalty-partnership revenue, including a co-branded Skyway Miles credit card

## Go-to-Market Strategy

Skyway leads with direct online sales through its website and app. It uses price promotions to stimulate demand on new routes and during softer travel periods. Partnerships with tourism boards help market destinations and leisure routes. Travel agents and the call centre provide assisted-booking channels for customers who prefer them.

## Team, Ownership, and Key Departments

Skyway was founded in 2015 and is privately held. It has approximately 5,500 employees and is profitable. About 150 people sit within the HQ and customer-facing teams that are a particular focus for the company.

Key departments include:

- Customer Support
- Airport and Ground Operations
- Network and Revenue
- Loyalty
- Policy and Compliance
- Flight Operations
- Digital (web and app)
- Finance
- Legal
- Marketing

## Market Position and Competitive Landscape

Skyway competes mainly with low-cost carriers and, on selected routes, with major network airlines. Its core competitive landscape includes two large legacy airlines and one ultra-low-cost carrier.

Skyway positions itself between these alternatives: **cheaper than legacy airlines and fairer than low-cost carriers**. Its strategic emphasis is transparent fares, understandable policies, reliable support when plans change, and a friendly service experience for leisure, family, and small-business travelers.
